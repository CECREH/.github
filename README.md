# Center of Excellence in Capacity-building for REsilient Housing (CECREH)

The **Center of Excellence in Capacity-building for REsilient Housing (CECREH)** is a HUD-funded research center based at **Texas Tech University**. We work with partners across the United States to advance climate resilience, equitable housing, and effective disaster recovery for vulnerable and underserved communities.

> This GitHub organization hosts open tools, data, and documentation that support CECREH’s research, partnerships, and training activities.

## About CECREH

CECREH was established with support from the **U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)** to address the challenges that vulnerable populations face before and after climate-driven disasters. Housed at **Texas Tech University in Lubbock, Texas**, the Center brings together a multidisciplinary team of engineers, social scientists, planners, policy scholars, and community partners.

Our work combines **data-driven analysis**, **human-centered research**, and **capacity-building** to support more just, resilient, and effective housing recovery systems.

---

## Mission and core pillars

CECREH’s mission is to make **resilience and equity central to housing recovery strategies** in the United States and beyond. Our efforts are organized around three core pillars:

1. **Assess climate risks for underserved communities**  
   - Characterize historic and future hazard exposure  
   - Identify communities facing the greatest climate-related housing risks  

2. **Build capacity for equitable disaster recovery**  
   - Improve how unmet housing needs are identified and measured  
   - Strengthen local and state capacity for long-term, equitable recovery  

3. **Develop tools and simulations for climate-resilient policy**  
   - Create models and decision-support tools for housing recovery  
   - Inform future-focused, climate-resilient plans and policies

---

## Research focus areas

CECREH’s research program is organized into three interconnected focus areas:

### 1. Climate risk threatening underserved communities

We investigate how **past and future climate hazards** affect housing in underserved communities, including:

- Modeling historic climate risk to understand where and how housing has been impacted  
- Modeling future climate risk under changing climate conditions  
- Identifying emerging needs to guide proactive policy and planning

### 2. Capacity building for unmet needs

We refine methods and tools for estimating **post-disaster housing recovery needs**, including:

- Developing benchmarks for housing recovery needs  
- Characterizing local government capacity for equitable housing recovery  
- Identifying the key levers that shape recovery outcomes over time

### 3. Resilient plans and policies

We design and test strategies to integrate **resilience, equity, and affordability** into housing systems, including:

- Analyzing how local capacity affects recovery trajectories  
- Using simulations to improve CDBG-DR and related housing recovery programs  
- Assessing resilience–cost tradeoffs for affordable and manufactured housing

For up-to-date project descriptions, see the **Research Projects** section of our main site.

## About this GitHub organization

This GitHub space complements the CECREH website and Documentation Hub by making **research artifacts and technical resources** openly available.

As this organization grows, you can expect to find:

- **Open-source analysis code and models**  
  - Hazard mapping and exposure modeling  
  - Recovery and agent-based simulation tools  
  - Policy scenario and planning tools

- **Data and reproducible workflows**  
  - Processed datasets and metadata (where sharable)  
  - Example notebooks and workflows for replicating analyses  

- **Documentation and training materials**  
  - Methodological notes, user guides, and technical reports  
  - Teaching and training resources on housing recovery and resilience  

Each repository will include its own **README**, **data documentation**, and **license information**.
